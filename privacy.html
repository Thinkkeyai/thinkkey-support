Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026

ThinkKey is an iPhone keyboard and companion app. This policy explains what happens to text and diagnostic information when you use ThinkKey.

ThinkKey works without Allow Full Access. Ordinary typing does not require network access.

Typing without cloud AI

Normal tap typing, swipe typing, suggestions, settings, and learned vocabulary operate on your device. ThinkKey does not send ordinary keystrokes, passwords, message history, or everything you type to its servers.

Without Full Access, shared companion-app settings and network AI features may be unavailable, but the keyboard continues to type.

Optional AI writing tools

ThinkKey can use supported Apple on-device language-model features when available. If an on-device result is unavailable, cloud AI can be used only when:

  1. You enable Allow Full Access for ThinkKey in iPhone Settings; and
  2. You enable Allow text I submit to be sent to AI in the ThinkKey companion app.

Text is sent only after you deliberately open an AI writing tool. A request can contain selected text or recent text before the cursor, the chosen writing action, language and tone, writing instructions, and up to three earlier generated results when you request another draft. ThinkKey does not submit text from secure password or verification-code fields.

Processing and retention

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Grammar and Shorten run on the device first, and nothing is sent for them unless the on-device result is unavailable.

Requests are encrypted in transit to ThinkKey's service on Cloudflare Workers, which sends them to one of two AI providers to generate the result:

  • Groq, running the GPT-OSS 120B model, is used first. ThinkKey's Groq account has Zero Data Retention turned on, so Groq does not keep the submitted text or the result. See Groq: your data in GroqCloud.
  • Cloudflare Workers AI, running Google's Gemma model, is used when Groq is unavailable or after your monthly premium allowance is used. Cloudflare states that Workers AI customer content is not used to train AI models or improve Cloudflare or third-party services without explicit consent. See Cloudflare Workers AI data usage and Cloudflare's privacy policy.

ThinkKey's service does not store raw submitted text or generated results. To apply fair-use limits, each request carries a random identifier that the app creates once per install; it is not derived from your device or identity and is never used for tracking or advertising. For that identifier the service keeps only request counts and the cost of AI used this month. Separately, it keeps daily totals (number of requests, which tools and models were used, response times and cost) that contain no text and no identifiers. Cloudflare and Groq may process limited network metadata as needed to operate and secure their services.

To keep the AI service for the genuine ThinkKey app, the companion app uses Apple's App Attest when you turn on AI text sharing. Your iPhone creates a key in its Secure Enclave and Apple certifies that it belongs to ThinkKey; ThinkKey's service checks Apple's certificate and gives this install a token for AI requests. This sends the install identifier above, the key's identifier and Apple's attestation, and no text. The service does not store them.

Do not submit passwords, authentication codes, payment details, health information, or other highly sensitive information to an AI tool.

Diagnostics

ThinkKey keeps limited crash reports locally on your device. Reports can include the component, app version, iOS version, device model, time, and non-text keyboard state. The reporter rejects typed text, messages, selections, passwords, and clipboard contents. Reports are not uploaded automatically; you choose whether to share them.

Accounts, advertising, and tracking

ThinkKey does not require an account for local keyboard features. It does not sell personal information, show third-party advertising, use advertising identifiers, or track activity across other companies' apps or websites.

Your controls

  • Turn cloud AI off with Allow text I submit to be sent to AI in ThinkKey.
  • Turn Full Access off in Settings › General › Keyboard › Keyboards › ThinkKey.
  • Clear local crash reports and reset settings from the companion app.

Children

ThinkKey is a general-audience productivity tool and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes and contact

If these practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before the changed practice is released. For privacy, support, or AI-output concerns, visit ThinkKey Support or email thinkkeyai@gmail.com. Do not include private message text, passwords, or authentication codes in a public report.
