ThinkKey Support

Get help with setup, typing, swipe input, themes, crash reports, or ThinkKey AI.

Report an issue

Include in your report

  • ThinkKey version and build number
  • iPhone model and iOS version
  • The app where the problem occurred
  • Clear steps that reproduce the problem
  • Whether Allow Full Access and AI text sharing were enabled
Protect your private information. Use fictional sample text. Do not post private conversations, passwords, verification codes, payment details, authentication tokens, or unedited screenshots containing personal information.

AI output concerns

Describe the selected tool, language, built-in or custom tone, and what was wrong with the result. A short fictional example helps us reproduce the issue safely.

Crash reports

Crash reports remain on your device until you choose to export them. In the ThinkKey companion app, open Settings › Data, Backup & Reset › Crash Reports. Review the report before sharing it.

Keyboard setup

  1. Open ThinkKey and choose Activate ThinkKey.
  2. Open Settings › General › Keyboard › Keyboards › Add New Keyboard.
  3. Select ThinkKey.
  4. Hold the globe key in a normal text field and select ThinkKey.

Normal tap and swipe typing works without Full Access. Full Access is needed for shared companion-app settings and optional cloud AI. Secure fields may switch to Apple's keyboard as required by iOS.

Response channel

Updates and replies appear on the public support issue you create. If your report contains sensitive information, remove it and describe only the behavior needed to reproduce the problem.
